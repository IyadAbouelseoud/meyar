البطاقة المطبوعة

The printed card

Sized in millimetres, not pixels, so it prints to wallet size. The face carries no name, no ID number and no result. Only the encrypted QR, the PIN instruction and the honesty line. The strip along the bottom echoes a passport’s machine-readable zone but holds only the envelope version and the issue date.

بطاقة الطبيب

Prescriber card

The card sits in the side rail of the order screen and never blocks signing. It leads with what to do, then shows the two phenotypes, the guideline line with its version, the coverage comb, and the CPIC-named alternatives as one-click suggestions. The chart-paper ground marks every clinician surface.

    What we refused, and what we did instead

    Default lookMeyar instead
    Purple-to-blue gradient hero on whiteJuniper and saffron from Aseer, flat fills, the scale as the hero
    Inter or system font everywhereReadex Pro for patients, Plex Sans Arabic for clinicians (the Saudi government platform face), Alexandria for headlines
    Three identical icon cardsOne instrument per gene, sized by what it says
    Glassmorphism, glow, neon on blackWarm kohl black with flat marks; texture is paper grain and chart-paper grid
    Thick coloured stripe on one side of a rounded cardStatus as shape + word + colour; indicator as a labelled chip
    Bouncy springs and fade-up on every blockOne calibration sweep per screen; decelerate, never overshoot
    Decorative sparklines and round-number “proof”Only logged counts, labelled Demo data; sourced numbers only from docs/sources.md
    Cream paper, serif display, terracotta accentGreen-tinted security paper, geometric Alexandria, terracotta kept only for the fast end of the scale
