Files (all in `hardware/blender/`, the scope lock):
- `build_animation.py` — imports F1's `build_renders.py` builders (no copy of the models), adds the turntable pivot, the T3 concept internals and the explode keys (baked per frame, so the saved `.blend` plays in the UI), the 16:9 cameras, and renders PNG frames to `frames/<shot>/` (git-ignored). Writes `anim/t3_anchors.json`: the 2D screen position of each callout anchor on the final, static frame.
- `art/make_video_art.py` — 1920 × 1080 caption overlays for each shot and one PNG per T3 callout, positioned from `t3_anchors.json`, in the Meyar fonts via headless Edge (reuses `make_art.py`).
- `encode_video.py` — ffmpeg: overlays captions and callouts (fade in), cross-fades the shots, encodes libx264 yuv420p CRF 18 + faststart → `renders/meyar-f2-kit-turntable-t3-exploded.mp4`. Asserts 1920 × 1080, 30 fps, h264, < 50 MB with ffprobe.

Honesty rules: T3 internals are generic shapes labelled as concept (reader module "chemistry not chosen"), no numbers anywhere apart from the mock kit ID, no result on any screen, callout claims taken only from DESIGN-PLAN.md design rationale (integrated screen, keyed slot, cartridge bound to kit ID, scanner, sealed button).

## Decisions made / open questions
- EEVEE, not Cycles: about 11 s against about 47 s a frame on the Intel Arc iGPU. Thin films (specimen bag, T3 cover glass) use blended alpha in EEVEE, because raytraced transmission rendered them grey and hid the screen.
- For the turntable, the kit is re-laid around the mailer, which is the pivot. The F1 still-life layout made the kit drift around the frame. The models themselves are F1's.
- T3 internals (tray, board, reader module, scanner) are illustrative shapes. The caption says "Internals are illustrative", and the reader callout says "chemistry not chosen". Callouts state design intent from DESIGN-PLAN.md only.
- The 0.5 s cross-fade briefly overlaps the two captions. Each shot keeps its own label, so no T3 pixel appears without "Concept render — not built".
- Callout Arabic strings are drafts for the C4 review.
- ffmpeg (already installed via winget) does the overlay and H.264 encode; it is a machine tool, not a repo dependency. Blender's own FFMPEG output is the fallback.

## Do not
- Do not use Blender 3D text for any label (no Arabic shaping); every label is an HTML-rendered PNG.
- Do not use single-surface transmissive planes (render black); see F1.
