## Decisions made / open questions
- The open Blender window's MCP server was not listening (localhost:9876 refused), so everything is built headless with `D:\blender.exe` from scripts. This is what makes the renders reproducible; open any `.blend` in the UI to inspect or tweak.
- Text is never Blender 3D text: Blender does not shape Arabic, so every printed surface is an HTML texture.
- T4 caption says "we found no cleared multi-gene near-patient PGx device" (targeted searches, not an exhaustive FDA/EUDAMED query), softer than "none exists".
- Mailer carries the WHO wording "Exempt human specimen". Saudi Post domestic acceptance of specimen mail is unconfirmed (its Alami list prohibits medical samples cross-border) — future deployment step.
- The numbers in DESIGN-PLAN.md (IATA PI650, CLSI, TAILOR-PCI, NICE costs…) have inline citations but no `docs/sources.md` rows. Do not put any of them on a slide until the sources owner adds rows. Several came from search snippets and are flagged there.
- Arabic strings on the props are drafts for the C4 native-speaker review.
- Step 1 on the mailer says "do not eat or drink first" without a minute count, because the 30-minute rule is unsourced.

## Do not
- Do not use PIL from `uv run --no-project` (not installed); compose runs inside Blender with numpy.
- Do not use single-surface transmissive planes (they render black); give film/glass a Solidify modifier or IOR ≈ 1.
