## Status and evidence
- T1 (saliva kit): kit design, software working (`kit/`). T3 (single-gene cath-lab unit) and T4 (multi-gene reader): **Concept render — not built**.
- Why T3 is roadmap, not a promise: in TAILOR-PCI, point-of-care CYP2C19-guided therapy did not reach statistical significance on its primary endpoint (HR 0.66, 95% CI 0.43–1.02, P=.06; Pereira NL et al., JAMA 2020;324(8):761–771, doi:10.1001/jama.2020.12443; row in `docs/sources.md`).
